# PHASE 1 – BRAINSTORMING & IDEATION

## Project Title

LegalEaseAI – AI-Powered Legal Document Generator

## 1. Problem Identification

Creating legal documents manually can be difficult and time-consuming for people who do not have legal knowledge.

Users may face difficulties in understanding legal formats, clauses, terminology and document structure.

## 2. Proposed Solution

LegalEaseAI is an AI-powered legal document generator that helps users create customizable legal document drafts based on their requirements.

The application collects information from the user and uses Generative AI to generate a structured legal document.

## 3. Project Objective

The main objective of LegalEaseAI is to simplify the process of creating basic legal document drafts using Artificial Intelligence.

The system aims to provide a simple and user-friendly interface for generating legal documents.

## 4. Target Users

- Individuals
- Students
- Freelancers
- Entrepreneurs
- Startups
- Small businesses
- Professionals

## 5. Proposed Features

1. Select the required legal document type.
2. Enter the parties involved.
3. Enter terms and conditions.
4. Enter the effective date.
5. Generate the legal document using Generative AI.
6. Display the generated document.
7. Allow the user to review the generated document.
8. Provide document download options.

## 6. Example Legal Documents

- Employment Contract
- Non-Disclosure Agreement
- Lease Agreement
- Rental Agreement
- General Legal Agreement

## 7. Technologies Considered

## 8. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a simple AI-powered application that can generate customizable legal document drafts from user-provided information.

## 9. Future Scope

## 10. Legal Disclaimer

The documents generated by LegalEaseAI are drafts for educational and informational purposes. They should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before official use.
